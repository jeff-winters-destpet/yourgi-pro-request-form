Mechanics this concept assumes (annotations, not UI)
  • Matched notifications: when a request lands, Pros who match it (service offered, coverage area, availability) get a push notification and come check the board. The board itself is the shared source of truth; the notification is just the doorbell.
  • Exclusive claim: pulling a card to Reviewing locks it to that Pro. Others see it grayed with the owner's name. (Server enforces the race: first claim wins.)
  • Claims expire: a claimed card auto-releases back to New Requests after 24h so requests don't rot in Reviewing. Shown on each claimed card.
  • Contact gating: the parent's phone/email stay masked until Accepted. Reviewing-stage communication happens through the platform (or concierge relay), which also protects the flat rate from off-platform deals.
  • Accepted = booked: accepting writes the appointment to the Pro's bookings list (right rail) at the quoted flat rate, and notifies the parent.
  • Escalation: cards aging past a threshold with no claim get flagged to the concierge team (today's Teams channel keeps working as the fallback + audit log).
